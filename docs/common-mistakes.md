# Common laser shop mistakes

A short, opinionated list of mistakes we see (and that public forums repeat endlessly). Fix these before you buy another machine.

## 1. Buying watts instead of a workflow

People shop peak watt marketing, then discover they needed exhaust, a rotary, LightBurn skill, or a different wavelength. Match [laser types](machines/laser-types.md) to the product you sell.

## 2. Skipping scrap grids

Copying a stranger’s speed/power screenshot onto a different tube, lens, and plywood brand wastes blanks. Use the [starter settings](materials/starter-settings.md) method.

## 3. Wrong or unverified eyewear

CO₂ glasses on a fiber, fashion tint on a UV, or defeated door interlocks. See [laser eye safety and fire](safety/laser-eye-safety-and-fire.md).

## 4. Ignoring cooling until the tube dies

Bucket leaks, ignored water-protect, and “it was fine this morning” heat fade. Read [CO₂ tube maintenance](machines/co2-tube-and-mirror-maintenance.md) and [CO₂ common problems](troubleshooting/co2-common-problems.md).

## 5. Assuming the red dot is the beam

Framing lies until calibrated — on CO₂ pointers and fiber galvos alike.

## 6. Lasering mystery plastics

PVC/vinyl incidents still happen. Identify materials; refuse unknowns.

## 7. Chasing color marks on the wrong tool

Expecting wide, repeatable stainless color from a basic Q-switch without accepting MOPA-level control (and still needing tests).

## 8. Leaving jobs unattended

The fire you “only stepped away for” is the expensive kind.

## 9. Underpricing file prep

Variable-name plaque batches are not “one plaque × N.” See [pricing laser work](business/pricing-laser-work.md).

## 10. Learning only from random short videos

Structured practice beats doom-scrolling. That is literally why we point to [Master LightBurn](https://course.beardedbuildsco.com/master-lightburn?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=cta&utm_campaign=common-mistakes).


## 11. Cleaning optics with the wrong attitude

Over-cleaning with abrasive paper ruins coatings; never-cleaning lets power quietly disappear. Match cleaning frequency to smoke load, and inspect with a flashlight on a schedule.

## 12. Measuring focus once forever

Change material thickness, lens, or rotary diameter and your old spacer story is wrong. Re-focus is cheaper than remakes.
