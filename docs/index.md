## How we write these pages

- We favor **safe, accurate guidance** over long lists of unverified numbers.
- Machine-specific power/speed numbers belong in *your* test grid — we explain the method and leave room for settings you have proven on your machine.
- Safety notes use callouts so they are hard to miss. Read them before you fire the laser.

## New to lasers?

1. Read [Laser types](machines/laser-types.md) so you know which machine does what.
2. Skim [What you can laser](materials/what-you-can-laser.md) — especially the materials you should never cut.
3. Walk through [LightBurn basics](lightburn/lightburn-basics.md) for your first job.
4. When something breaks, check [Common LightBurn errors](troubleshooting/common-lightburn-errors.md).
